Privacy Policy

IBD Clinical Decision Support — Last updated: April 2026

Summary

This app collects no personal data, no health data, and no usage data of any kind. Nothing leaves your device. There are no accounts, no logins, no analytics, and no third-party services of any kind embedded in the app.

Who This App Is For

IBD Clinical Decision Support is intended exclusively for licensed healthcare professionals (physicians, advanced practice providers, pharmacists, and trainees under supervision). It is not intended for use by patients or the general public.

Data Collection

We collect no data. Specifically:

  • No personal information is collected or stored.
  • No patient data is entered, stored, or transmitted.
  • No health or medical records are accessed.
  • No device identifiers, IP addresses, or usage statistics are collected.
  • No HealthKit or any other Apple health framework is used.
  • No analytics or crash reporting services are integrated.
  • No third-party SDKs that collect data are included.
  • The app does not require an account or login of any kind.
  • The app functions entirely offline; no network requests are made during use.

Clinical Information Disclaimer

This app provides evidence-based clinical decision support based on published guidelines from the American College of Gastroenterology (ACG 2025) and the American Gastroenterological Association (AGA 2024–2025). It does not replace clinical judgment, specialist consultation, or current prescribing information. It is not for use in emergencies. Some recommendations may reflect off-label use. The developers accept no liability for clinical decisions made using this tool. All treatment decisions remain the sole responsibility of the treating clinician.

FDA Regulatory Status

This tool qualifies as Non-Device Clinical Decision Support Software under Section 520(o)(1)(E) of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act, as amended by Section 3060(a) of the 21st Century Cures Act (FDA Final Guidance, January 2026). No FDA clearance or approval is required or has been sought.

Children

This app is rated 17+ and is not directed at children under the age of 17. No data from minors is collected.
